# Engineering Internship — Intermarc Global Pty Ltd (IMG)

**Placement:** ENGG454 | **Duration:** 9 Dec 2024 – 28 Feb 2025 (12 weeks)
**Mentor:** Tharaka Madduma Liyanage, Manager – Engineering Services

IMG is a building services / mechanical engineering consultancy; it also holds a patent for an
amplitude-modulation AC machine controller and is developing a permanent-magnet-free electric motor
for EV/eAviation applications.

The placement covered three pieces of work, not just one:

## 1. Compliance audit — kitchen exhaust system, 45 Clarence Street
Audited an installed kitchen exhaust system against **AS 1668.1:2015**, **AS 1668.2:2012**, and the
National Construction Code. Calculated the required exhaust airflow for the hood types present:
- Hood Type 3 (sidewall): **3,017.6 L/s**
- Hood Type 4 (island): **767.6 L/s**

These figures were used to check whether the installed fan was actually adequate for the space.

## 2. Building Management Systems — 3M Building, 1 Rivett Road
Introduced to Building Management Systems using the **Alerton Systems** platform. Studied the
protocols and architecture behind Direct Digital Control (DDC): BACnet, Modbus, M-Bus, DALI, and
KNX.

## 3. Plant operations — ACA Building, 66 King Street
A 15-storey building with central plant on Level 14: 2 air-cooled Carrier chillers, 2 chilled-water
pumps, 2 hot-water pumps, ventilation fans, and tenancy occupancy relays. Observed a live
chiller-fault troubleshooting session and studied wet/dry-pipe sprinkler activation.
